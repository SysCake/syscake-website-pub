About

Engineers and researchers, building in layers.

We started in technology transfer and university research. We still work that way: understand the problem deeply, prove the idea, then build it to last.

Syscake was founded in Rome in 2022. Today we design and build software for public institutions, research centers and companies, and we ship products of our own.

Our name says how we work. Like a cake, a good system is made of layers — infrastructure, data, models, agents, product — and each one has to hold the next. The pixel cherry on top is the part people see.

Artificial intelligence runs through all of it. We use it where it does real work, we measure it, and we keep it under our clients' control.

How we work

From idea to production, in the open.

A transparent, iterative method: you see working software early, and you own the result at the end.

  1. Step 01

    Co-design

    Workshops to find the real need and turn it into a technical plan.

  2. Step 02

    Prototype

    A working MVP, fast, to validate choices before scaling them.

  3. Step 03

    Production

    Full-stack delivery, from UX to security, on cloud or on-premise.

  4. Step 04

    Handover

    Support, training and knowledge transfer, so your team owns the result.
