Morsiqo

An AI agent that takes the order — and sees it through.

Takeaway and delivery ordering through chat. An AI agent guides each customer from the menu to payment and keeps them posted until the food arrives; the restaurant follows everything from a real-time app.

In developmentWhatsApp · WebAI agent
About the product

Customers already chat on WhatsApp. With Morsiqo they can order there too, talking to an AI agent that knows the restaurant's menu, prices, opening hours and capacity.

The agent does the work of a good waiter: it explains dishes, suggests, builds the cart, takes payment and sends updates. When something needs a human — a special request, a full kitchen — it asks the staff, who decide with one tap from the merchant app.

An agent, not a menu bot

It understands free text, follows the conversation and handles changes of mind.

From menu to payment

Cart, secure payment, pickup or delivery slot — all inside the chat.

The restaurant stays in control

A real-time app for incoming orders, decisions, menu and opening hours.

Built for many restaurants

A multi-tenant platform: each restaurant has its own menu, rules and staff.
