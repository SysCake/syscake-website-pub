Chroniq

Your meetings, kept close.

A meeting recorder for macOS. It transcribes on your Mac and turns the conversation into a summary, decisions and action items — written by the model you choose, local or cloud.

Available · v0.5.1macOSOn-device AI

About the product

Chroniq records your meetings and keeps everything on your Mac. Transcription runs locally; the summary is written by the language model you pick, so sensitive conversations never have to leave your machine.

Local transcription

Speech-to-text runs on Apple silicon, without sending audio anywhere.

Your model, your choice

Use a local model for privacy or a cloud model for power — per meeting.

Decisions and actions

Every meeting becomes a summary, a list of decisions and the action items that follow.

Private by default

Recordings and transcripts stay on your Mac unless you decide otherwise.
