Videat

Where to eat, as told by video reviews.

A guide to restaurants and bars in Italy built on YouTube food reviews. AI finds every venue, score and moment, so you can watch exactly what the creator said.

In developmentWeb · iOS · AndroidLLM extraction
About the product

Food creators review thousands of places on YouTube, but the knowledge is buried in hours of video. Videat watches for you: it extracts each venue, the verdict and the exact moment it is discussed, and puts them on a map.

From video to map

Every venue mentioned in a review becomes a place you can find.
