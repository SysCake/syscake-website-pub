04 — How we work

From idea to production, in the open.

A transparent, iterative method: you see working software early, and you own the result at the end.

  1. Step 01

    Co-design

    Workshops to find the real need and turn it into a technical plan.

  2. Step 02

    Prototype

    A working MVP, fast, to validate choices before scaling them.

  3. Step 03

    Production

    Full-stack delivery, from UX to security, on cloud or on-premise.

  4. Step 04

    Handover

    Support, training and knowledge transfer, so your team owns the result.
